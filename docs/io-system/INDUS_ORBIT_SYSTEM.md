# What Indus Orbit is

Status: product and system interpretation grounded in the current application and plans, 1 August 2026.

## Core proposition

Indus Orbit is a people-centred Indian intelligence, learning, trust, and action network. Its purpose is to reduce the distance between a person with potential and the people, knowledge, compute, capital, institutions, and opportunities needed to act.

The name describes the intended structure: many people and institutions retain their own identity and agency while becoming easier to discover, trust, support, and coordinate within a shared orbit. The platform should create useful gravity, not centralised control. Technology is infrastructure for human capability, not the principal.

## Who is at the centre

The current product recognises youth/builders, founders, experts, investors, diaspora, partners, and researchers. Each actor both contributes and receives:

| Actor                     | Can contribute                                         | Should receive                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Youth and builders        | effort, local knowledge, experimentation, peer support | learning, mentors, verified opportunity, tools, discovery    |
| Founders                  | missions, jobs, execution, problems worth solving      | talent, expertise, capital, partners, governed compute       |
| Experts and mentors       | judgment, skills, credibility, review                  | high-signal people and work, visible impact, safe boundaries |
| Investors and sponsors    | capital, sponsorship, institutional access             | evidence-backed people, teams, progress and outcomes         |
| Diaspora                  | global networks, market access, knowledge, capital     | accountable paths to contribute to India                     |
| Partners and institutions | infrastructure, cohorts, capacity, legitimacy          | coordination, reach, evidence and measurable impact          |
| Researchers               | analysis, methods, synthesis, public knowledge         | trustworthy observations, collaborators and distribution     |

No actor should be reduced to an engagement metric, lead, model user, or source of donated capacity. Consent, attribution, privacy, meaningful control, support, and appeal are product requirements.

## The reinforcing loops

1. **Identity and trust:** a person establishes a profile, relationships, demonstrated work, endorsements, and appropriate verification.
2. **Knowledge and learning:** people publish, study, teach, research, and make practical knowledge easier to use.
3. **Conversation and coordination:** direct and group communication turns discovery into relationships and decisions.
4. **Action:** Missions, Chapters, events, mentorship and other programmes turn intention into observable work.
5. **Intelligence and compute:** I/O Port gives governed access to commercial, Indian, open-source, owned, sponsored, donated, BYOK, and local capacity.
6. **Evidence and return:** receipts, progress, outcomes and human review improve trust, allocation, learning and future opportunity.

These loops explain why the Discord-like request is about spatial clarity and collaboration, not copying Discord. They also explain why I/O Port must be inside the Indus Orbit trust and work system while retaining a stricter technical authorization boundary.

## I/O Port within Indus Orbit

I/O Port is intended to be India’s people-centred intelligence exchange: one governed entry point to multiple providers and capacity classes. It is comparable to an open router at the protocol level, but its differentiation must be operationally real:

- route provenance understandable by a member;
- capacity sourced through partnerships, owned/rented infrastructure, BYOK, and explicitly governed donations;
- evidence-backed model origin, serving location, retention/training terms and price;
- Indian languages, speech, documents and public-interest workloads as first-class needs;
- budgets, sponsorship and credits that can widen access without obscuring cost;
- human review, support and recourse for consequential automated work;
- I/O Terminal turning model access into permissioned, inspectable work rather than only chat completion.

“India’s own OpenRouter” is a useful category explanation, not a sufficient claim. A credible I/O Port must prove routing independence, provider diversity, policy, cost integrity, reliability, privacy boundaries, India-specific capacity, and member value.

## Product boundaries

- Human direct messages remain human records. Prompts, model outputs, tool traces and terminal output do not belong in `direct_messages`.
- Community membership or vouching can inform trust and access, but cannot replace workspace authorization, provider contracts, workload policy or sandbox isolation.
- A Mumbai database does not make a global provider request India-resident. Storage, processing and model-serving location must be disclosed separately.
- Sponsored or donated compute is opt-in, revocable, provenance-labelled and restricted to eligible workloads. It is never presented as equivalent to contracted private capacity.
- Model/provider fallback cannot silently change material quality, price, region, retention or training policy.
- The system may recommend and automate, but people retain meaningful visibility and control over consequential actions.

## Brand translated into product behavior

The canonical identity is **“The General Intelligence Company of India”**, never “for India.” The concise brand line is **“India Thinks Together.”** It expresses the operating thesis—people, institutions, models and capacity becoming more capable through cooperation—without suggesting an aerospace company. Public identity and stationery use `indusorbit.com`; `jri.ai` is not an Indus Orbit identity domain.

Indigo provides structure and trust; parchment provides a calm working surface; saffron marks deliberate attention and action. Scientific credibility comes from evidence, timestamps, units, uncertainty and reproducibility—not decorative technical language. The voice should be ambitious without making claims that the system cannot yet prove.

The spatial application model is a persistent Orbit rail, contextual navigation, main workspace and optional inspector. That geometry can support People, Learn, Action, Messages and I/O while keeping their data and permissions distinct.

## Technical architecture in one sentence

A React/TanStack application uses Supabase Auth, RLS-governed Postgres, narrow RPC/Edge Function trust boundaries and authorized realtime delivery; I/O adds a registry-driven policy router and, later, dedicated gateway/runner infrastructure for long-lived streaming and terminal work.

## Success measures

Product success should combine technical and human measures:

- time from discovery to a useful trusted connection;
- learning completion and demonstrated skill, not passive consumption;
- mission and mentorship outcomes, not message volume;
- equitable access to useful capacity by geography, language and role;
- route success, latency, cost accuracy, policy compliance and explained fallback;
- sponsored-capacity benefit with consent and auditable allocation;
- safety incidents, appeals, recovery time and trust repair;
- the member’s ability to explain who or what acted, on whose authority, with which data and at what cost.
